# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Bread Client project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Trolling, personal attacks, or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sharing malware, exploits, or content intended to harm others' devices or accounts
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (this repository, its issue tracker, and the associated Discord server), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
