Privacy

A small data footprint.

Draft for review · Last updated 30 September 2026

This page describes the current project site. It is not a substitute for legal advice, and the controller identity, contact address, retention periods, and jurisdiction must be completed before a public commercial launch.

What this site collects

This static site does not run analytics, advertising pixels, account sign-in, contact forms, or third-party embeds. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or device profiles. GitHub may process ordinary server and security logs when you visit GitHub-hosted pages or follow a GitHub link; that processing is governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy notice.

What the desktop client needs

Bread Client stores local settings, instance files, logs, and credentials needed for the features you request. Microsoft account and Minecraft authentication are handled by the relevant providers. The app should only send requests needed for the selected feature, and its diagnostics must redact access tokens and refresh tokens.

Third-party services

The site embeds none. The desktop app can connect to services such as Microsoft, Minecraft, Modrinth, CurseForge, and GitHub when a feature requires them. Review each provider's terms before enabling or using that service.

Your choices

You can avoid the site entirely, clear local browser storage, and remove local Bread Client data through the app or your operating system. If a future release adds analytics, a form, payments, or embedded media, this page and the consent flow must be updated first.
