Cookies

No tracking by default.

Draft for review · Last updated 30 September 2026

The current Bread Client site is static. It does not set analytics, advertising, social, or behavioural cookies and it does not load third-party scripts or iframes. Because there is no non-essential cookie category to turn on, the site does not show a cookie consent wall.

What may still exist

GitHub Pages and GitHub may use infrastructure and security mechanisms outside this project's control. Follow the provider's own notices for those services. The download acknowledgement on this page is a form control only; it is not stored in a cookie.

When consent would be needed

If analytics, advertising, social embeds, behavioural measurement, or other non-essential device storage is added, it must be blocked until the visitor gives specific consent. The choice must be clear, separate by purpose where appropriate, and as easy to withdraw as it was to give.

Why this approach

European guidance distinguishes technically necessary storage from non-essential tracking. The project currently chooses not to add the latter. Re-check this policy before adding a form, payments, embedded video, a newsletter, or a metrics service.
